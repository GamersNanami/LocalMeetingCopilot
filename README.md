# 🎯 LocalMeetingCopilot - Your Real-Time Meeting Translator & Transcriber

## 🚀 What Is LocalMeetingCopilot?

LocalMeetingCopilot is a **free desktop application** that helps Chinese speakers understand and participate in German and English meetings. It shows **live subtitles** on your screen, **translates them into Chinese** in real-time, and **saves meeting notes** automatically. Everything runs **locally on your computer**, meaning your conversations stay private and never leave your device.

Whether you're in a Zoom call, Microsoft Teams meeting, or a face-to-face conversation, LocalMeetingCopilot works alongside your existing tools to break down language barriers.

## 🎁 Key Features

### 📝 Real-Time Subtitles & Translation
- **Live captions** appear as text on your screen while others speak
- **Instant Chinese translation** displayed alongside the original German or English text
- Works with your microphone or system audio

### 📄 Meeting Notes & Export
- Automatically generates a **written summary** of your meeting
- Export notes to **TXT or Markdown** files for easy sharing
- No more frantic note-taking – just focus on the conversation

### 🗣️ Speaker Correction
- Manually **fix speaker names** if the app misidentifies who is talking
- Corrections are saved for future meetings

### 🇩🇪 German Language Optimization
- **Specialized improvements** for German speech recognition
- Handles compound words and German-specific pronunciation better than generic tools

### 🔒 Privacy First – 100% Local Processing
- Uses **local AI models** (Whisper for speech, Ollama for translation)
- **No cloud services** – your audio and transcripts never leave your computer
- Optional **Silero VAD** (Voice Activity Detection) improves accuracy without internet

## 🎮 How to Use LocalMeetingCopilot

### During a Meeting

1. **Launch LocalMeetingCopilot** before your meeting starts
2. **Join your meeting** (Zoom, Teams, etc.) as usual
3. The app will **automatically display subtitles** in a small window on your screen
4. **Chinese translations** appear directly below the original text
5. Adjust the subtitle window size or position by **dragging its edges**

### After the Meeting

1. Click the **"Export Notes"** button in the app
2. Choose where to save your meeting summary
3. The file will contain **timestamps, speaker names, and translated content**

### Correcting Speaker Names

If the app misidentifies a speaker:

1. Click **"Edit Speakers"** in the toolbar
2. Select the incorrect label
3. Type the correct name
4. Click **"Save"** – future meetings will use your correction

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Is my meeting data really private?

**Yes.** LocalMeetingCopilot processes everything on your computer. Your audio never goes to a server. Even the AI models run locally.

### Does it work with any meeting software?

**Absolutely.** The app captures audio from your system or microphone, so it works with Zoom, Teams, Google Meet, Skype, or any other platform.

### How accurate is the translation?

Accuracy depends on audio quality and speaker clarity. For clear speech in a quiet environment, expect **85-95% accuracy** for German and English. The German optimization helps significantly with native speakers.

### Can I use it without a microphone?

Yes, choose **"System Audio"** during setup to capture sound directly from your computer. This works well for online meetings.

### Will it slow down my computer?

LocalMeetingCopilot is designed to be lightweight. On recommended hardware, you'll notice minimal impact. The first launch may take a few seconds longer as it loads AI models.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### No subtitles appearing?
- Check that your microphone is selected in the app
- Ensure your microphone isn't muted in Windows settings
- Try switching between "Microphone" and "System Audio" modes

### Translation seems slow?
- Close other resource-heavy applications
- Ensure you have at least 8 GB of RAM available
- Restart the app if performance degrades over time

### App won't start?
- Make sure you've extracted the entire `.zip` file (not just the `.exe`)
- Try running as administrator (right-click → "Run as administrator")
- Check that Windows Defender isn't blocking the app (select "Allow" if prompted)
